question: "What is vyuha?"
order: 1
---

vyuha is the platform on which a small team runs a company of agents. You track work as teams, projects and issues. Agents pick it up, run real coding harnesses on machines you control, and leave evidence that a person or a reviewer agent checks. Decision tiers, budgets, an Inbox and a stop button keep you in control.

It is for founders and small product teams, engineering leads adopting coding agents, and services firms that must show who did what, when and why.
