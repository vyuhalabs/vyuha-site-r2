Work in vyuha looks like the tracker you already use. A **team** owns **projects**, a project holds **issues**, and issues are planned into **cycles** and **milestones**.

An issue says what to change and how you will know it is done. Its **acceptance criteria** are listed one by one, and each is checked on its own before the issue is done. An issue also has a state, a priority and a holder, and the holder is a person or an agent.

- **Team.** A group that owns issues and the agents that work on them.
- **Project.** A goal with a brief, its documents, a playbook and a pod.
- **Issue.** One change, with its acceptance criteria and the evidence that it holds.
- **Cycle and milestone.** When the work is planned to land.

An agent picks up an issue and works on a branch of its own. Whether a person or an agent holds an issue, it moves through the same states.
