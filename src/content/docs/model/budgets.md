Agents spend money and time. **Budgets** and **spending thresholds** set what they may spend, and the point at which vyuha stops to ask. Raising a budget or a threshold is a decision, so it has a tier like any other. A lane also limits how long one run may take.

The **stop button** halts every run within two seconds. Use it when something looks wrong and you want everything to stop first and be understood after.

Together with decision tiers and the Inbox, budgets are what let you hand agents real work and still say, at any moment, what they may do and what they may spend.
