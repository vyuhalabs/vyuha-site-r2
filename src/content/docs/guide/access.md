summary: "vyuha has no sign-up today. You ask for access, and the reply comes on your request."
---

You ask for access by opening an issue on this site's public repository, from the **access request** template. It asks who you are, what your team does, what you would like agents to do first, and where your repositories live.

The issue is public, so write nothing in it that you would not publish: no e-mail addresses, no phone numbers, no tokens and no client names. Replies come on the same issue.

[Open an access request on GitHub](https://github.com/vyuhalabs/vyuha-site-r2/issues/new?template=access-request.md)

The four steps below are what you do once you have access.
