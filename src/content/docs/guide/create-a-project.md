A project is a goal with a team of agents behind it. Give it a name, write a short brief and choose the playbook it follows.

A good brief says what you are building, who it is for, what is in and what is out, and how you will know it worked. If the brief leaves something open that the work needs, the lead agent does not guess. It asks you in one decision, in your Inbox, with each question and the answer it recommends.

Once you have answered, the lead plans the work and files the issues, each with its acceptance criteria. The project starts at the first stage of its playbook.
